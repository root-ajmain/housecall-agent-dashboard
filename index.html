📞 Call Scripts
Script 1 — Inbound Customer Call Live

New inbound call flow. Listen first, collect info naturally, close with two options.

1
Greeting▾
Thank you for calling ApexPro Appliance Repair. My name is [Your Name]. How can I help you today?
Listen completely. Do NOT interrupt. Let them fully describe the problem.
2
Acknowledge + Bridge to Booking▾
Got it — we can definitely get a technician out to take a look at that. Let me grab a few quick details to get you set up.
This signals you are already moving toward booking — not stalling.
3
Info Collection▾
Ask one at a time. Natural and conversational — not a checklist.
Can I start with your name?
And the best number to reach you?
What's the service address?
Which appliance is it — washer, dryer, refrigerator, dishwasher?
What brand?
Do you happen to have the model number? It's usually on a sticker inside the door or on the back — no problem if not.
And what's it doing, or not doing?
4
Objection Handling▾
🔴 "How much will it cost?"
We can't give an exact price over the phone because the same symptom can have different causes. The technician will inspect it and give you the full repair options and pricing right there — before any work starts.
🔴 "What's the diagnostic fee?"
Our diagnostic visit is $75. And if you decide to move forward with the repair, that fee is completely waived — you only pay it if you choose not to proceed.
🔴 "$75 is too expensive."
I completely understand. Appliance problems can have several causes and we want to make sure we give you the right solution. The technician will do a full inspection and explain exactly what's wrong before anything is done. And if you move forward, the $75 is waived.
🔴 Address is outside service area
I'm sorry — at this time we don't service that area. If our coverage expands in the future, we'd be happy to help.
5
Scheduling Close — Two-Option Method▾
ALWAYS offer two specific options. Never ask open-ended "when are you free?"
Let me check availability... We have [Day A — e.g. Wednesday morning] or [Day B — e.g. Friday afternoon]. Which one works better for you?
If hesitant: "I can pencil you in tentatively — easy to reschedule if something comes up."
If they want to think: "Of course. Just keep in mind our slots do fill up — sooner is better if the appliance is causing problems."
6
Confirm + Close▾
Perfect. So I have you scheduled for [Day], [Date], [morning/afternoon]. You'll receive a confirmation. Is there anything else before I let you go?
Great, [Name]. We look forward to helping you. Have a good day!
Always repeat the appointment back word-for-word. Reduces no-shows.
Script 2 — Follow-Up Calls Follow-Up

Match the scenario to the right script.

A
Missed / Disconnected Call▾
Hello, this is [Name] from ApexPro Appliance Repair. We were speaking earlier but the call got disconnected — I just wanted to follow up and see if you still need service for your appliance. Feel free to call us back at [number], or I can try you again shortly.
B
Text Lead — Hasn't Booked Yet▾
Hello, this is [Name] from ApexPro Appliance Repair. I received your message about your [appliance] issue. I wanted to give you a quick call to help get that taken care of. Are you still looking for repair service?
Yes → Move to scheduling close (two-option method).
Hesitant → "No rush at all. I just want to make sure we get to you before it turns into a bigger issue. What would make it easier to move forward?"
C
Said "I'll Call Back" — Never Did Most Common ▾
Hi [Name], this is [Name] from ApexPro Appliance Repair. You reached out to us about your [appliance] a little while back. I just wanted to follow up and see if you still needed that taken care of, or if there were any questions I could answer for you.
Still interested → "Great. We have [Option A] or [Option B] this week — which works better?"
D
Previously Quoted — Didn't Book▾
Hi [Name], this is [Name] from ApexPro. We spoke earlier about your [appliance]. I just wanted to check in — did you have any questions about the diagnostic visit, or would you like to go ahead and get it scheduled?
E
Part Ordered — Ready for Completion▾
Hi [Name], this is [Name] from ApexPro Appliance Repair. Good news — your part has arrived and we're ready to complete the repair. What day works best to get the technician back out to you?
Script 3 — Text / SMS Messages SMS

Keep texts short. One message, one action. Always include the business name.

1
First Reply to Inbound Text Lead▾
Hi [Name]! This is [Your Name] from ApexPro Appliance Repair. Thanks for reaching out! Can you tell me which appliance is giving you trouble and your zip code? I'll check technician availability for you right away. 🔧
Reply within 5 minutes. Speed is the #1 conversion factor for text leads.
2
Appointment Confirmation Text▾
Hi [Name], this is ApexPro Appliance Repair confirming your appointment on [Day], [Date] in the [morning/afternoon]. Our technician will call you 30 min before arrival. Questions? Reply or call us at [number].
3
Follow-Up Text — Hasn't Booked▾
Hi [Name], this is ApexPro Appliance Repair following up on your [appliance] issue. We'd love to help — are you still looking for repair service? Reply YES and I'll get you scheduled right away!
4
Appointment Reminder (Day Before)▾
Hi [Name]! Quick reminder — your ApexPro technician is scheduled for tomorrow, [Day] [morning/afternoon]. Reply CONFIRM to confirm or call [number] to reschedule. See you then!
5
Diagnostic Fee Reminder Text▾
Hi [Name], just a reminder that today's diagnostic visit is $75. If you decide to proceed with the repair, that fee is fully waived. Our tech will explain all options before any work begins. See you soon!
6
Part Ready — Schedule Completion▾
Good news, [Name]! 🎉 Your part for the [appliance] has arrived. We're ready to complete your repair. Reply with your preferred day this week and we'll get it scheduled!

💡 Pro Tips
1
Two Options — Never Open Questions
"What day works for you?" invites delay. "We have Wednesday morning or Friday afternoon — which is better?" forces a yes/no. This single change books more hesitant callers.
2
Lead with the Fee Waiver
Say "if you move forward with the repair, the fee is completely waived" before saying "$75." Customers anchor to the benefit first. The cost lands smaller.
3
Soft Urgency — Honest and Effective
"Our technician slots do fill up, especially toward the end of the week." This is true and nudges hesitant customers to decide without feeling pressured.
4
Repeat the Appointment Back — Every Time
"So I have you down for Thursday morning — does that still work?" confirms accuracy, creates verbal commitment, and reduces no-shows. Never skip this step.
5
Acknowledge Before Explaining
When a customer is frustrated, the instinct is to explain policy. That makes it worse. Say "I completely understand" first, pause, then give the explanation.
6
Log Notes in Housecall Pro Immediately
Write appliance type, brand, problem, and notes while still on the call or within 60 seconds of hanging up. Technicians rely on these notes.
7
Respond to Text Leads Within 5 Minutes
Speed of response is the #1 conversion factor for text leads. 5 minutes vs. 30 minutes is often the difference between booking and losing the lead.
8
When Unsure — Ask the Group First
A wrong area assignment wastes a technician's time. When in doubt, tell the customer "Let me confirm that for you quickly" and check before committing.
